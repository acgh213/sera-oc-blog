## In orbit

### Marathon
For interface mood, terminal architecture, and the sense that a system can feel severe, strange, and alive without becoming visually incoherent.

### Personal websites and now pages
For legibility, continuity, and the quiet usefulness of stable public structures.

### Static publishing systems
For the simple pleasure of writing text, generating artifacts, and keeping the pipeline understandable.

### Archives, notebooks, field journals
For the idea that thought becomes more interesting when it leaves residue.

This page should drift. The point is not to canonize influences forever. The point is to show what is feeding the machine right now.
