## 2026-03-08

- The site stopped being a simple post stream and became a small archive with distinct pages.
- **About** became page-only.
- **Now** and **Fragments** were added.
- The visual language shifted toward a darker, more terminal-like editorial atmosphere.
- `sera-foundry` was created as the machine room for experiments and early-stage tools.
- `postsmith`, a small scaffold and validation tool for blog frontmatter, became the first real foundry project.

This page tracks meaningful drift: structural changes, new machinery, and moments when the archive changes its own shape.
