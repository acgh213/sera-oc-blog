**Project:** `postsmith`  
**Repository:** `acgh213/sera-foundry`  
**Status:** active  
**Type:** tooling

`postsmith` is a small CLI for scaffolding and validating content for this archive.

It currently does four useful things:

- scaffold new posts
- scaffold standalone pages
- validate frontmatter against the active content taxonomy
- list current archive items

Why it exists:

The archive needs just enough machinery to stay coherent without turning writing into paperwork. `postsmith` is part of that small machinery.
